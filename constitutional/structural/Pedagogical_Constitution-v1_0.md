# ⭐ **2 — Constitutional Principles (PC‑Series)**

### **PC‑1 — Altitude Preservation**
All pedagogical artifacts must remain within A3–A5 and may not cross into expressive or governance altitude.

### **PC‑2 — Membrane Sovereignty**
Pedagogical artifacts may not absorb expressive geometry or governance logic.

### **PC‑3 — Deterministic Sequencing**
All pedagogical processes must be reversible, non‑recursive, and structurally deterministic.

### **PC‑4 — Non‑Activation Clause**
Pedagogical artifacts may not activate NDH geometry, holonomy, lineage membranes, or governance altitude.

### **PC‑5 — Structural Neutrality**
Pedagogical artifacts must remain geometry‑neutral and expressive‑neutral.

### **PC‑6 — Reversible Routing**
All routing between SOL‑phases must be reversible and membrane‑aligned.

### **PC‑7 — Clarification Priority**
Clarifications must precede gating, failure, and spiral logic.

### **PC‑8 — Invariant Integrity**
SOL‑7 invariants must remain sealed and cannot be modified by expressive or governance constitutions.

# ⭐ **3 — Constitutional Articles (A‑Series)**

### **A‑1 — Article of Structural Sovereignty**
The Pedagogical Constitution is sovereign over all structural artifacts and cannot be overridden by expressive or governance constitutions.

### **A‑2 — Article of Sequencing Order**
Pedagogical sequencing must follow:

```
Clarifications → SOL‑7 → Gating Logic → SOL‑8 → SOL‑9
```

### **A‑3 — Article of Membrane Boundaries**
Pedagogical membranes must enforce:

- non‑absorption  
- non‑authority  
- altitude preservation  

### **A‑4 — Article of Failure Safety**
SOL‑8 must operate within structural altitude and may not invoke expressive or governance logic.

### **A‑5 — Article of Spiral Containment**
SOL‑9 must remain reversible and may not generate altitude drift.

### **A‑6 — Article of Template Foundations**
G0‑Primer (T1) and G0‑Grammar (T2) must adhere to all PC‑Series principles.

### **CR‑4 — Adjacency Safety**
