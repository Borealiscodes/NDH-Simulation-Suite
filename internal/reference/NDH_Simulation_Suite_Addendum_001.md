# **📄 Addendum — NDH‑Simulation‑Suite Clarification (v1.0)**  
### *Standalone Internal Note*

This addendum clarifies the role of **NDH‑Simulation‑Suite** within the unified NDH architecture.

---

## **1. Updated Role Definition**

NDH‑Simulation‑Suite is confirmed as the **high‑dimensional mathematical quarantine layer** of NDH.  
It is used for:

- stability tensor prototypes  
- resonance and drift‑field modeling  
- dimensional explainers (30D–42D)  
- PDE evolution scaffolding  
- nondual algebra experiments  
- pre‑Constellation validation of mathematical structures

Simulation‑Suite is **not** a trauma‑geometry layer, **not** a research altitude, and **not** a runtime environment.

---

## **2. Relationship to Other NDH Subsystems**

- **NDH‑TIDS**  
  Comparative meta‑architecture.  
  Simulation‑Suite is *not* part of TIDS.

- **TISD**  
  Trauma‑informed subsystem.  
  Simulation‑Suite does *not* host trauma geometry.

- **NDH‑Research‑Pilot**  
  Mid‑altitude research platform.  
  Simulation‑Suite predates Research‑Pilot and remains distinct.

- **NDH‑PLATFORMS**  
  Runtime layer.  
  Simulation‑Suite is pre‑runtime and non‑operational.

---

## **3. Charter Context**

The earlier **Simulation Suite Sandbox Charter (v1.0)** remains valid as a **historical governance instrument**, created before:

- NDH‑Research‑Pilot existed,  
- NDH‑TIDS was altitude‑separated,  
- Constellation governance stabilized.

Simulation‑Suite continues to serve as the **quarantine altitude for high‑dimensional math**, but is no longer the sole sandbox for architectural validation.

---

## **4. Current Status**

Simulation‑Suite is:

- stable  
- non‑activating  
- non‑runtime  
- mathematically focused  
- pre‑Constellation  
- correct for high‑dimensional prototypes

It remains an essential internal layer for NDH’s mathematical and structural development.
