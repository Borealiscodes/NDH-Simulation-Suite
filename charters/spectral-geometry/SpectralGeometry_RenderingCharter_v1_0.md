## ⭐ **3 — Rendering Rules**

### **Rule 1 — Creative Geometry Only**  
Rendering must remain descriptive, generative, and non‑executing.

### **Rule 2 — PRECL‑Safe Surfaces**  
Rendering must not modify PRECL‑collapsed surfaces.

### **Rule 3 — No VM‑Grade Rendering**  
No solvers, no operator families, no VM‑VEX bindings.

### **Rule 4 — Rasa‑Manifold Safety**  
Rendering must remain:

- sealed  
- altitude‑bounded  
- non‑recursive  
- non‑diagnostic  

### **Rule 5 — Federated Constellation Context**  
Rendering must remain inside Simulation‑Suite’s federated envelope.

### **Rule 6 — Reversible Rendering**  
All rendering must be reversible:

\[
\mathcal{R}^{-1}(R_{\text{spec}}) = G_{\text{spec}}
\]

### **Rule 7 — Drift Neutrality**  
Rendering must not introduce spectral drift.
