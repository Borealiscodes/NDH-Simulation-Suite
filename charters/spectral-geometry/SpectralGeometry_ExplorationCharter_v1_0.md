## ⭐ 3 — Exploration Rules

### Rule 1 — Creative Geometry Only  
Spectral geometry must remain descriptive, generative, and non‑executing.

### Rule 2 — No VM‑Grade Solvers  
No eigenvalue solvers, no operator families, no VM‑VEX bindings.

### Rule 3 — Rasa‑Manifold Safety  
Rasa‑Manifold spectral exploration must remain:

- sealed  
- altitude‑bounded  
- non‑recursive  
- non‑diagnostic  

### Rule 4 — Federated Constellation Context  
Spectral geometry must remain inside Simulation‑Suite’s federated envelope.

### Rule 5 — PRECL‑Safe Rendering  
Spectral geometry must not modify PRECL‑collapsed surfaces.

### Rule 6 — Reversible Routing  
All spectral mappings must be reversible.

### Rule 7 — Drift Neutrality  
Spectral geometry must not introduce spectral drift.
