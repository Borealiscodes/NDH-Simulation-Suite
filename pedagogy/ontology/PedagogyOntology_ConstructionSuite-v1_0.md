## ⭐ 1 — Suite purpose

The Pedagogy Ontology Construction Suite:

- defines how ontology tiles are built  
- binds Attenborough Axis (macro meaning ecology) and Nye Axis (micro semantic mechanics)  
- ensures narrative clarity across the ontology series  
- maintains Simulation‑Suite altitude discipline  
- provides machine‑readable indexing for tile loaders  
- supports both **basic** and **advanced** ontology tiles  

It is the **construction backbone** for the Ontology‑Series.

---

## ⭐ 2 — Construction principles

### **2.1 Axis integration**

- **Attenborough Axis:** macro semantic/meaning framing  
- **Nye Axis:** micro ontology mechanism clarity  
- Both axes must appear in every ontology tile.

### **2.2 Narrative roles**

Each ontology tile declares one:

- orientation  
- exploration  
- deepening  
- interaction  
- synthesis  
- advanced semantic/epistemic ontology  

### **2.3 Codex gravity layer**

- implicit  
- non‑narrativized  
- stabilizing  
- never a pedagogy axis  

### **2.4 Safety constraints**

- non‑activation  
- reversible  
- Simulation‑Suite altitude only  
- no governance altitude  
- no sealed geometry
