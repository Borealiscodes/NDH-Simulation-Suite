# ⭐ **1 — Suite Purpose**

The Pedagogy Topology Construction Suite:

- defines how topology tiles are built  
- binds Attenborough Axis (macro topology) and Nye Axis (micro topology)  
- ensures narrative clarity across the topology series  
- maintains Simulation‑Suite altitude discipline  
- provides machine‑readable indexing for tile loaders  
- supports both **basic** and **advanced** topology tiles  

This suite is the **construction backbone** for the Topology‑Series.

---

# ⭐ **2 — Construction Principles**

### **2.1 Axis Integration**
- **Attenborough Axis:** macro topology framing  
- **Nye Axis:** micro topology mechanism clarity  
- Both axes must appear in every topology tile  

### **2.2 Narrative Roles**
Each topology tile must declare one:

- orientation  
- exploration  
- deepening  
- interaction  
- synthesis  
- advanced structural topology  

### **2.3 Codex Gravity Layer**
- implicit  
- non‑narrativized  
- stabilizing  
- never a pedagogy axis  

### **2.4 Safety Constraints**
- non‑activation  
- reversible  
- Simulation‑Suite altitude  
- no sealed geometry  
- no governance altitude
