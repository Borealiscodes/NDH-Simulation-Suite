## ⭐ **1 — Tone Constitution Overview**

This standard governs **three narrative surfaces**:

- **Attenborough Tone** — macro conceptual framing  
- **Nye Tone** — micro mechanism clarity  
- **Tile Narrative Surface** — tile‑specific teaching voice  

These tones must appear in **every pedagogy tile**, regardless of domain.

---

## ⭐ **2 — Attenborough Tone (Macro Narrative Standard)**

### 🜁 Definition  
Attenborough Tone expresses **macro‑scale conceptual ecology**:

- wide‑field framing  
- relational behavior  
- adjacency as landscape  
- stability as story  
- drift as pattern  

### 🜁 Required Characteristics  
- soft macro curvature  
- gentle ecosystem‑scale perspective  
- non‑directive conceptual framing  
- narrative breadth without activation  

### 🜁 Forbidden Zones  
- persona  
- urgency  
- evaluative tone  
- mechanistic over‑precision (belongs to Nye)  

### 🜁 Cross‑Domain Application  
- **Ecology:** ecosystem behavior  
- **Topology:** continuity & curvature envelopes  
- **Ontology:** semantic ecology  

---

## ⭐ **3 — Nye Tone (Micro Narrative Standard)**

### 🔬 Definition  
Nye Tone expresses **micro‑scale mechanism clarity**:

- precise motions  
- micro‑energetics  
- micro‑adjacency  
- micro‑interference  
- mechanism‑level behavior  

### 🔬 Required Characteristics  
- crisp but gentle micro‑explanation  
- mechanism‑first clarity  
- reversible micro‑logic  
- non‑directive precision  

### 🔬 Forbidden Zones  
- macro metaphor (belongs to Attenborough)  
- persona  
- activation  
- evaluative framing  

### 🔬 Cross‑Domain Application  
- **Ecology:** micro‑interference & micro‑energetics  
- **Topology:** micro‑continuity & micro‑curvature  
- **Ontology:** micro‑semantic mechanics  

---

## ⭐ **4 — Tile Narrative Surface Standard**

### 🌿 Definition  
The Tile Narrative Surface is the **tile‑specific teaching voice** that blends:

- Attenborough Tone  
- Nye Tone  
- the tile’s narrative role  
- the tile’s conceptual behavior  

### 🌿 Required Characteristics  
- soft academic curvature  
- gentle conceptual geometry  
- reversible narrative motion  
- domain‑specific clarity  
- no activation, no persona  

### 🌿 Narrative Role Binding  
Each tile’s narrative surface must align with its role:

- **orientation**  
- **exploration**  
- **deepening**  
- **interaction**  
- **synthesis**  

Guided Link: **Narrative Role Arc**

---

## ⭐ **5 — Tone Invariants (Cross‑Domain Rules)**

### 🔷 5.1 Macro–Micro Parity  
Every tile must contain:

- one Attenborough Tone section  
- one Nye Tone section  
- one Tile Narrative Surface  

### 🔷 5.2 Axis Alignment  
Attenborough ↔ macro axis  
Nye ↔ micro axis  

### 🔷 5.3 Codex Gravity Neutrality  
Tone must never:

- activate codex  
- narrativize codex  
- personify codex  

### 🔷 5.4 Domain Parity  
Tone rules apply identically to:

- **Ecology Tiles**  
- **Topology Tiles**  
- **Ontology Tiles**
