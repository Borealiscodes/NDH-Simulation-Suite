# ⭐ **1 — Population Constitution Overview**

Tile population is governed by:

- **Population Sequencing**  
- **Axis Binding**  
- **Codex Gravity Binding**  
- **Narrative Role Alignment**  
- **Machine‑Readable Requirements**  

These rules apply to **every tile** in:

- Ecology  
- Topology  
- Ontology  

---

# ⭐ **2 — Population Sequencing Standard**

### 🔷 2.1 Required Order  
Every domain must populate tiles in this sequence:

1. **Orientation**  
2. **Exploration**  
3. **Deepening**  
4. **Interaction I**  
5. **Interaction II**  
6. **Synthesis (Finale)**  

This is the **Narrative Role Arc**, and it is mandatory.

### 🔷 2.2 Domain Parity  
All three domains must follow the same sequence:

- **Ecology**  
- **Topology**  
- **Ontology**  

### 🔷 2.3 No Batch Population  
Tiles must be populated **one at a time**, in order.  
Batch population is forbidden because it breaks:

- narrative clarity  
- axis alignment  
- codex gravity neutrality  
- Simulation‑Suite altitude discipline  

---

# ⭐ **3 — Axis Binding Standard**

### 🔷 3.1 Macro Axis Binding  
Every tile must bind to the **Attenborough Axis** for macro tone.

### 🔷 3.2 Micro Axis Binding  
Every tile must bind to the **Nye Axis** for micro tone.

### 🔷 3.3 Axis Binding Invariants  
Axis binding must be:

- explicit  
- stable  
- reversible  
- domain‑aligned  

### 🔷 3.4 Axis Binding Parity  
Axis binding rules apply identically across:

- Ecology  
- Topology  
- Ontology  

---

# ⭐ **4 — Codex Gravity Binding Standard**

Codex gravity is the stabilizing layer beneath all pedagogy tiles.

### 🔷 4.1 Gravity Binding Requirements  
Every tile must:

- declare its codex gravity anchor  
- maintain gravity neutrality  
- avoid narrativizing codex  
- avoid activating codex  

### 🔷 4.2 Gravity Binding Parity  
Gravity rules apply identically across all domains.

---

# ⭐ **5 — Narrative Role Alignment Standard**

Every tile must align its population with its narrative role:

- **Orientation** → gentle introduction  
- **Exploration** → motion, drift, curiosity  
- **Deepening** → mechanism, structure  
- **Interaction I** → relational behavior  
- **Interaction II** → adjacency behavior  
- **Synthesis** → unified conceptual closure  

This alignment is mandatory.
