# ⭐ **1 — Bridge Overview**

This bridge integrates two constitutional standards:

- **Narrative Tone Standard** — governs Attenborough, Nye, and tile narrative surfaces  
- **Tile Population Standard** — governs sequencing, axis binding, codex gravity, and machine‑readable population rules  

The bridge ensures:

- tone appears *in the correct place*  
- tone appears *in the correct order*  
- tone aligns with *population sequencing*  
- tone aligns with *axis binding*  
- tone aligns with *codex gravity neutrality*  
- tone aligns with *domain parity*  

This is the “glue layer” that makes the pedagogy lane coherent.

# ⭐ **2 — Tone ↔ Population Integration Rules**

### 🔷 2.1 Tone Must Follow Population Sequence  
Every tile must express:

- Attenborough Tone  
- Nye Tone  
- Tile Narrative Surface  

**in alignment with its narrative role**:

- orientation  
- exploration  
- deepening  
- interaction  
- synthesis  

Tone cannot precede or exceed the tile’s population role.

Guided Link: **Narrative Role Arc**

---

### 🔷 2.2 Tone Must Bind to Axis Requirements  
Tone must follow axis binding rules:

- Attenborough ↔ macro axis  
- Nye ↔ micro axis  

This ensures tone is not free‑floating or misaligned.

Guided Link: **Axis Binding**

---

### 🔷 2.3 Tone Must Respect Codex Gravity  
Tone must:

- declare codex gravity anchor  
- remain neutral  
- remain non‑narrativized  
- remain non‑activated  

Guided Link: **Codex Gravity Binding**

---

### 🔷 2.4 Tone Must Maintain Domain Parity  
Tone rules apply identically across:

- **Ecology Tiles**  
- **Topology Tiles**  
- **Ontology Tiles**  

No domain may drift into another domain’s tone.

---

# ⭐ **3 — Population ↔ Tone Enforcement Rules**

### 🔷 3.1 One Tile at a Time  
Tone and population must be applied **tile by tile**, never in batches.

This prevents:

- narrative collapse  
- axis misalignment  
- codex drift  
- sequencing violations  

Guided Link: **Tile Population Sequencing**

---

### 🔷 3.2 Tone Must Be Present Before Tile Finalization  
A tile is not considered “populated” until:

- Attenborough Tone  
- Nye Tone  
- Tile Narrative Surface  

are fully present.

This ensures narrative completeness.

---

### 🔷 3.3 Tone Must Be Altitude‑Consistent  
Tone must remain at Simulation‑Suite altitude:

- soft academic  
- gentle curvature  
- reversible  
- non‑activating  

No tile may introduce higher‑altitude narrative structures.
