## ⭐ **1 — Suite Purpose**

The Construction Suite:

- defines how ecology tiles are built  
- binds Attenborough Axis (macro) and Nye Axis (micro)  
- ensures narrative clarity across the series  
- maintains Simulation‑Suite altitude discipline  
- provides machine‑readable indexing for tile loaders  
- supports both basic and advanced tile families  

This suite is the **construction backbone** for the Ecology‑Series.
